4.1 Vessel and pools
--------------------
(Parameter / Value)
Main vessel                        : 15.0 m ID, 14.5 m high, 316LN, 40 mm wall
Guard vessel                       : 15.6 m ID, 0.3 m gap, leak detectors in the gap
Roof                               : 2.5 m shielded slab; large and small rotating plugs
Primary sodium                     : 1,250 t: hot pool 620 t, cold pool 560 t, core and diagrid 70 t
Sodium per MWt                     : 0.53 t/MWt, close to BN-600 (Superphénix ≈ 1.1). This is the
                                     main responsiveness knob
Free surface                       : Hot pool 95 m²; cold pool annulus 45 m²
Mixing time constants              : Hot pool 58 s (model as 40 s + 18 s nodes); cold pool 52 s plus
                                     8 s transport from IHX outlets to pump inlets
Heat capacity, sodium plus internals
    ≈ 2,400 MJ/K

Pool levels: the hot pool sits above the cold pool, and that head difference is what pushes sodium
through the IHXs.

(Level relation / Formula)
Head difference : ΔL = 1.5 m × (Q/Q₀)²
Hot pool        : L_mean + 0.32 × ΔL
Cold pool       : L_mean − 0.68 × ΔL
Mean level      : +0.26 m from 375 °C isothermal to 100%; +0.64 m from 230 °C to 100%

----------------------------------------------
(Parameter / Value)

Arrangement               : Primary on shell side flowing down; secondary in tubes flowing up
Primary side              : 1,782 kg/s each; 545 °C in from hot pool, 375 °C out to cold pool

4.4 Cover gas
-------------
(Parameter / Value)
Gas, volume            : Argon, 320 m³ above the pools at 100%
Pressure               : Setpoint 0.120 MPa; auto band 0.115–0.125
Limits                 : Alarm low 0.105, high 0.140; trip 0.160; never below 0.102 (air ingress)
Heatup and cooldown    : Sodium expansion moves the gas volume by ≈ 90 m³ between 230 °C and full
                         power. With a fixed gas inventory, cooldown alone would pull pressure down
                         to ≈ 0.074 MPa, so argon has to be admitted on cooldown and vented to decay
                         tanks on heatup
Normal activity        : Ar-41 (110 min half-life)
Fuel failure signature : Xe-133, Xe-135, Kr-85m, Kr-88
Decay tanks            : 3 × 50 m³, 72 h hold-up before the stack †


4.5 Decay heat removal (DRACS)
------------------------------
(Parameter / Value)
Loops       : 4 independent: sodium-sodium exchanger in hot pool, natural-circulation sodium loop,
              sodium-air exchanger, stack
Capacity    : 20 MWt per loop at 550 °C hot pool; Q = 20 MW × ((T_hot − 30) / 520)^1.4 †
Standby     : Dampers 5% open, 0.3 MW loss per loop keeps the loop warm
Dampers     : 2 per loop, motor-operated from essential boards; fail as-is (OD-6) because the motors
              can't move them without power; local handwheel 90 s each
Coping time : With no heat sink at all, the pool rises 200 K in ≈ 5 h after a trip from 100%


4.6 Primary purification
------------------------
Two 100% cold traps, 8 kg/s each †, trap wall 125 °C. Plugging temperature normal ≤ 125 °C, alarm
150 °C, LCO 180 °C. Oxygen meter online; tritium and carbon by sampling.

5.2 Sodium-water reaction protection
------------------------------------
* Micro
    Water leak rate: below 0.05 g/s
    Detected by: Hydrogen trend over grid-hours
* Small
    Water leak rate: 0.05–10 g/s
    Detected by: Section hydrogen meter within minutes
* Intermediate
    Water leak rate: 10 g/s – 2 kg/s
    Detected by: Acoustic leak detection, seconds
* Large
    Water leak rate: above 2 kg/s
    Detected by: Pressure rise and rupture discs, immediate

Hydrogen signal: a 1 g/s water leak adds +0.28 ppm at its section outlet meter and +0.036 ppm at the
loop outlet meter about 10 s later. Background is ≈ 0.06 ppm at 100% and 0.02 ppm at hot standby,
drifting ±0.01 ppm per grid day †.

Large leak, automatic sequence: rupture discs burst at 1.2 MPa, then separator tank, pump trip and
loop isolation, water side isolated in ≤ 5 s and blown down, nitrogen purge, sodium drained to the
dump tank, and reactor runback RB-1.

6.1 Construction
----------------
(Parameter / Value)
Arrangement                  : 3 SGs × 8 identical sections (1-1 … 3-8). Each section has an
                               evaporator (EV), superheater (SH) and reheater (RH) module; straight
                               tubes, sodium on shell side
Materials                    : 2¼Cr-1Mo evaporator; 9Cr-1Mo superheater and reheater †
Section duty                 : 99.5 MWt: EV 62.6, SH 21.2, RH 15.7
Loop duty                    : EV 501, SH 170, RH 125 MWt
Sodium path                  : 520 °C in, split 57.6% SH and 42.4% RH in parallel, both leave at 445
                               °C, mix, then EV, out at 320 °C
Water path, per section      : Feed 36.5 kg/s at 16.0 MPa, 240 °C; EV outlet 14.6 MPa, 355 °C (15 K
                               superheat); SH outlet 14.0 MPa, 505 °C
Reheat path, per section     : 31.9 kg/s from 2.75 MPa, 292 °C to 2.6 MPa, 505 °C
Pinch                        : ≈ 20 K where boiling starts (sodium 360 °C, saturation 340 °C)
Hot-end approach             : 15 K (sodium 520 °C, steam 505 °C)
Section isolation            : Sodium valves 60 s; feed and steam valves ≤ 5 s; water dump valve; N₂
                               purge
One section isolated at 100% : Loop capacity −12.5%; reactor −4.2 %FP

6.3 Splitter valves (steam generator desk)
------------------------------------------
(Parameter / Value)
CV-2A, CV-2B      : Hydraulic control valves; 20 s full stroke; 5 s fast close; fail as-is on loss
                    of hydraulic pressure †
Losses            : Fully open 0.10 MPa at full branch flow; equal-percentage trim; throttled to 50%
                    open costs ≈ 0.8 MPa, about 6 MWe on that branch †
NRV-2A, NRV-2B    : Swing check valves; close on reverse ΔP above 0.02 MPa
Turbine desks see : Valve position, branch flow, check valve state, both header pressures. Repeaters
                    only
Auto mode         : Holds position. Does nothing on a loop loss


6.4 Reheat routing (OD-4)
-------------------------
* A
    Reheater modules: SG1 sections 1–8 plus SG2 sections 1–4 (12 modules)
    Capacity: 1.5 loops' worth
* B
    Reheater modules: SG3 sections 1–8 plus SG2 sections 5–8 (12 modules)
    Capacity: 1.5 loops' worth

Each reheater group (SG1, SG2-A, SG2-B, SG3) has steam-side isolation valves, 30 s stroke. Each hot
reheat line has an attemperator spray fed from the feed pump interstage; sprays can only cool.

Why this matters: the splitter moves loop 2's main steam between turbines, but reheater capacity is
fixed by pipework. Push steam towards B and B's reheat runs cold while A's runs hot. Isolating a
single SG2 section nudges the balance by about 2 K in each direction, which a good steam generator
desk trims out with a small split change.

Reading it: 50/50 keeps reheat perfect but leaves B at 33%, a hair above its 30% minimum load, where
the next disturbance trips it. Sending everything to B looks balanced, yet it loses 22 MWe and runs
both reheat lines out of specification. The right answer is 60–70% of loop 2 to B: about 3 MWe lost,
B comfortably above minimum, reheat within limits. Crews who understand the plant will find it, and
crews who don't will pick one of the obvious answers.


6.6 Startup and low-load behaviour
----------------------------------
Once-through modules have no drum. Steam temperature is set by the ratio of feed flow to sodium
flow.

(Item / Value)
Startup separator          : One per SG between EV and SH; separator level 2.0 m; drains to
                             deaerator when hot, condenser when cold
Minimum evaporator flow    : 25% (73 kg/s per SG) through the separator
Switch to once-through     : EV outlet superheat ≥ 15 K held for 5 min, around 30–35% load
Water admission interlocks : Sodium ≥ 250 °C; feed temperature ≥ sodium − 80 K
Density-wave oscillation   : EV flow below 20% with EV sodium inlet above 400 °C: section flow
                             oscillates with a 6–10 s period, growing 5% per cycle until flow is
                             raised or sodium cooled †
