9. CONTROL, PROTECTION AND OPERATING LIMITS
====================================================================================================

9.1 Operating modes
-------------------
* 1
    Name: Power
    Conditions: Above 5 %FP
* 2
    Name: Startup
    Conditions: k_eff ≥ 0.99, up to 5 %FP
* 3
    Name: Hot standby
    Conditions: ≥ 1,000 pcm subcritical; core inlet 350–380 °C
* 4
    Name: Shutdown
    Conditions: ≥ 1,000 pcm subcritical; 200–350 °C
* 5
    Name: Refuelling
    Conditions: 230 ± 20 °C; ≥ 3,000 pcm subcritical; rotating plugs unlocked

No sodium that is meant to stay liquid goes below 180 °C in any mode.


9.2 Part-load program
---------------------
Core inlet is held at 375 °C throughout. With r = P/Q: core outlet 375 + 175r; secondary cold 375 −
55r; secondary hot 375 + 145r; main steam min(505, secondary hot − 15r). Below about 25 %FP the turbines are off and steam goes to the
bypass: the 20%, 10% and 5% rows are bypass or house-load states, because 178, 86 and 42 MWe are under the
150 MWe minimum stable load (§7.1) and their steam is below the reheat trip temperature.

9.3 Who controls what
---------------------
* Neutron power, core outlet temperature
    Owner: Reactor desk (MCR)
    Main actuator: RR, shim banks
    Upset by: Inlet temperature, flow, burnup, anomalies
* Core flow, P/Q = 1.00
    Owner: Primary desk (MCR)
    Main actuator: PP speeds
    Upset by: Power changes, pump trips
* Core inlet temperature
    Owner: Secondary sodium desk (SGCR)
    Main actuator: SP speeds
    Upset by: Feed flow and temperature, loop loss
* SG sodium outlet, main steam temperature
    Owner: Steam generator desk (SGCR) with feedwater desk
    Main actuator: Feed flow per SG, sprays
    Upset by: Turbine load, feed temperature
* Loop 2 split, reheat balance
    Owner: Steam generator desk
    Main actuator: CV-2A/2B, sprays, reheater isolation
    Upset by: Turbine loads, section isolations
* Header pressure, MWe
    Owner: Turbine A and B desks (TCR)
    Main actuator: Control valves, bypass
    Upset by: Reactor power, grid frequency
* Feed supply, deaerator, hotwell
    Owner: Feedwater desk (FWCR)
    Main actuator: Feed pump speeds, crosstie
    Upset by: Load, pump trips
* Boards, EDGs, MVAr
    Owner: Electrical desk (ECR)
    Main actuator: Breakers, AVR setpoints
    Upset by: Grid events, trips
* Trace heating, cold traps, argon
    Owner: Sodium services desk (NSCR)
    Main actuator: Zone heaters, trap flows
    Upset by: Faults, drift
* Water and sodium chemistry, activity
    Owner: Chemistry desk (NSCR)
    Main actuator: Sampling, polishing, dosing
    Upset by: Leaks, fuel failures

In reactor leads mode (default), the shift supervisor sets reactor power and the turbines hold
header pressure. In turbine leads mode (OD-5), the supervisor sets MWe: header pressure falls, the
SG and feedwater desks follow, the core inlet cools, and reactor power rises through inlet feedback
about 1–2 minutes later while the reactor desk trims. It's slower and needs four desks to cooperate.


9.4 Automatic controllers, deliberately worse than a crew
---------------------------------------------------------
* Rod auto
    Holds: Core outlet 550 ±2 K using RR only
    Weakness built in: Never moves shims; stops and alarms when RR leave the 250–750 mm band; reacts
      after thermocouples move
* Primary flow auto
    Holds: P/Q at 1.00
    Weakness built in: 10 s filter on power, so P/Q swings ±5% on fast changes; ignores a pump trip
* Secondary flow auto
    Holds: Core inlet 375 ±3 K
    Weakness built in: Drives all three loops equally even with isolated sections; 60 s integral
      time
* Feed auto
    Holds: Main steam 505 ±3 K
    Weakness built in: Overshoots ±8 K on 10% steps; no turbine feedforward; off in separator mode
* Attemperation auto
    Holds: Hot reheat ≤ 505 °C
    Weakness built in: Can only cool
* Splitter auto
    Holds: Valve position
    Weakness built in: Holds it and nothing more
* Turbine auto, pressure mode
    Holds: Header 13.5 ±0.2 MPa, plus FCR response when contracted
    Weakness built in: Ignores the reheat load limit; A and B don't coordinate
* Bypass auto
    Holds: Opens at header + 0.4 MPa
    Weakness built in: Slow; pressure swings ±0.3 MPa
* Cover gas auto
    Holds: 0.115–0.125 MPa
    Weakness built in: Cycles on heatup; vents without checking activity
* Trace heating auto
    Holds: Zone setpoints
    Weakness built in: Can't see failed heaters or stuck contactors
* Deaerator and hotwell auto
    Holds: Levels
    Weakness built in: Robust: naturally stable by design
* EDGs and sequencer
    Holds: Essential boards
    Weakness built in: Robust, but restores essential loads only
* Load-follow master
    Holds: Dispatcher MW at exactly 1 %/min
    Weakness built in: Follows dispatch straight into violations


9.5 Reactor protection
----------------------
Primary shutdown system (PSS) logic is 2-out-of-3 on independent channels.

* PR high flux
    Normal: 100%
    Alarm or first action: 105%
    Trip: 115%
* PR high flux, low setpoint (below 10% permissive)
    Normal: –
    Alarm or first action: –
    Trip: 25%
* Period, SR and WR below 10%
    Normal: Infinite
    Alarm or first action: 30 s rod block
    Trip: 10 s
* Flux rate
    Normal: 0
    Alarm or first action: –
    Trip: +10 %FP/s, or −5 %FP/s (Phénix-style)
* P/Q, above 5 %FP
    Normal: 1.00
    Alarm or first action: 1.05
    Trip: 1.12
* Core outlet, mixed
    Normal: 550 °C
    Alarm or first action: 565
    Trip: 580
* Assembly outlet vs expected
    Normal: 0 K
    Alarm or first action: +20 K alarm; +30 K rod block
    Trip: +45 K on 2 adjacent thermocouples
* Core inlet
    Normal: 375 °C
    Alarm or first action: 390 high, 360 low
    Trip: 405 high, 345 low; the low trip is inhibited in Modes 4 and 5, in Mode 3 with every
      PSS rod fully inserted, and in Mode 2 below 350 °C during heatup; it is armed in Mode 1 and
      above 5 %FP, and every inhibit is annunciated
* Hot pool level vs program
    Normal: 0 m
    Alarm or first action: ±0.25 m
    Trip: −0.50 m
* Cover gas
    Normal: 0.120 MPa
    Alarm or first action: 0.140
    Trip: 0.160
* DND
    Normal: Background
    Alarm or first action: ×3
    Trip: ×20
* Primary pumps
    Normal: 3 running
    Alarm or first action: 1 lost: RB-2
    Trip: 2 lost
* Secondary loops
    Normal: 3 running
    Alarm or first action: 1 lost: RB-1
    Trip: 2 lost
* Both turbines lost
    Normal: –
    Alarm or first action: RB-3 to bypass capacity
    Trip: If bypass unavailable
* Large sodium-water reaction
    Normal: –
    Alarm or first action: Loop isolation plus RB-1
    Trip: –
* All 11 kV boards lost
    Normal: –
    Alarm or first action: –
    Trip: Immediate
* Seismic
    Normal: –
    Alarm or first action: 0.04 g
    Trip: 0.08 g
* Manual
    Normal: –
    Alarm or first action: –
    Trip: 2 buttons in MCR, 1 in the backup control room

Secondary shutdown system, diverse sensors: flux 118%, P/Q 1.15 using pump-speed-derived flow, core
outlet 590 °C, manual. Each SSR also has a Curie-point latch that releases at 640 °C local sodium,
with ≈ 20 s thermal lag.

* RB-1
    Trigger: One secondary loop lost
    Target: 64 %FP
    Rate: 30 %/min: shim drive-in at 10 mm/s plus flow program
* RB-2
    Trigger: One primary pump lost
    Target: 60 %FP
    Rate: 60 %/min
* RB-3
    Trigger: One or both turbines lost
    Target: Bypass-limited power
    Rate: 20 %/min

Rod withdrawal blocks: period below 30 s; P/Q above 1.05; any assembly rod block; reactivity balance
beyond ±50 pcm; RR out of band during a bank move; more than one bank selected.


9.6 Operating limits
--------------------
* LCO-1
    Limit: SSS: at least 8 of 9 rods operable
    If exceeded: Hot shutdown
    Completion (grid time): 6 h
* LCO-2
    Limit: DRACS: at least 3 of 4 loops
    If exceeded: Restore or shut down
    Completion (grid time): 72 h
* LCO-3
    Limit: EDGs: at least 3 of 4
    If exceeded: Restore or shut down
    Completion (grid time): 72 h
* LCO-4
    Limit: DND: at least 4 of 6 channels
    If exceeded: ≤ 30 %FP
    Completion (grid time): 12 h
* LCO-5
    Limit: Reactivity balance within ±50 pcm
    If exceeded: No power increase; explain the deviation
    Completion (grid time): 1 h
* LCO-6
    Limit: Primary plugging temperature ≤ 180 °C
    If exceeded: ≤ 60 %FP and restore
    Completion (grid time): 24 h
* LCO-7
    Limit: IHX secondary minus primary pressure ≥ 0.2 MPa
    If exceeded: Isolate the loop
    Completion (grid time): 1 h
* LCO-8
    Limit: Every running SG section has a working hydrogen meter
    If exceeded: Isolate the section
    Completion (grid time): 4 h
* LCO-9
    Limit: Feed O₂ ≤ 10 ppb and cation conductivity ≤ 0.5 µS/cm
    If exceeded: ≤ 50 %FP
    Completion (grid time): 24 h
* LCO-10
    Limit: Ramp ≤ 1 %/min above 40% (≤ 3 %/min with supervisor approval, logged)
    If exceeded: Report
    Completion (grid time): –
* LCO-11
    Limit: Hot pool rate of change ≤ 1.5 K/min, trips excepted
    If exceeded: Report and fatigue review
    Completion (grid time): –
* LCO-12
    Limit: Cover gas Xe-133 below limit †
    If exceeded: ≤ 30 %FP and locate failed fuel
    Completion (grid time): 8 h

10.1 Rooms and walking times
----------------------------

    Desks: Reactor, primary, shift supervisor
    Walk from MCR †: –
* Electrical control room (ECR)
    Desks: Electrical
    Walk from MCR †: 40 s; EDG rooms a further 90–120 s
* Steam generator control room (SGCR)
    Desks: Secondary sodium, steam generator
    Walk from MCR †: 60 s
* Sodium services control room (NSCR)
    Desks: Sodium services, chemistry
    Walk from MCR †: 60 s
* Turbine control room (TCR)
    Desks: Turbine A, turbine B
    Walk from MCR †: 75 s
* Feedwater control room (FWCR)
    Desks: Feedwater
    Walk from MCR †: 90 s
* Backup control room (BCR)
    Desks: Emergency shutdown panel
    Walk from MCR †: 150 s, separate building

    Desks: Local dampers only

Place rooms so that no two desks that have to talk during a fault can see each other.


10.2 Desk contents
------------------
* Reactor (RO)
    Controls: RR 1–3; shim rod or bank select A/B/C, in/out; rod mode; SSR latch and withdraw (key);
      ECP calculator with 1/M plot; trip buttons
    Indications: SR, WR log power, PR, period; 30 rod positions; 331-position core map with
      temperature and deviation views; mixed outlet and inlet; P/Q ×3; reactivity balance; DND ×6;
      quadrant tilt; first-out annunciator
* Primary (PO)
    Controls: PP1–3 start, stop, speed, mode; pony motors; IHX shutters ×6; cover gas admit and
      vent; DRACS dampers ×8 and mode
    Indications: Pump speed, flow, current, bearing and winding temperatures, vibration, check valve
      state; hot and cold pool levels and temperatures; level difference; cover gas pressure and
      temperature; IHX inlet and outlet ×6; DRACS temperatures and airflow; guard vessel leak
      detector
* Shift supervisor (SS)
    Controls: Mode key; control mode; plant load master; reserve contracts FCR-N, FCR-D and aFRR;
      dispatcher line; room phones; authorisations for bypasses, isolations and fast ramps;
      emergency declaration
    Indications: Plant mimic; gross and net MWe; frequency; dispatcher instructions; LCO status;
      event log; end-of-shift report
* Secondary sodium (SSO)
    Controls: SP1–3; IHX secondary isolation valves; buffer tank argon; loop drain; SWR protection
      reset
    Indications: Loop flows, temperatures and pressures; pump data; buffer and dump tank levels;
      rupture disc status; cell leak detectors; IHX pressure difference
* Steam generator (SGO)
    Controls: Section isolation ×24 (grouped, individual override); water dump and N₂ purge; MSIV-1
      and MSIV-3; SG relief valves; CV-2A/2B; reheater isolation groups; sprays A/B; separator mode
      and level ×3
    Indications: Hydrogen meters ×27; acoustic detectors ×24; section EV, SH and RH outlet
      temperatures; section feed flows; SG outlet pressure and temperature; header A/B pressure;
      splitter branch flows and check valve state; hot reheat A/B; separator levels; oscillation
      monitor
* Turbine A, Turbine B (TO-A, TO-B)
    Controls: Latch and trip; speed target and run-up; synchroscope and breaker close; load setpoint
      and rate; speed, load, pressure or limit mode; FCR response enable; HP and LP bypass; vacuum
      pumps; CW pumps; hood sprays; turning gear; oil pumps; AVR
    Indications: Speed, MWe, MVAr, frequency; valve positions; stop-valve steam conditions; bypass
      flows; condenser pressure; CW temperatures; hood temperature; vibration on 8 bearings;
      eccentricity; differential expansion; thrust; oil pressure and temperature; rotor stress
      margin; reheat load limit; splitter repeaters
* Feedwater (FWO)
    Controls: Condensate pumps ×6; polishing in or bypassed; LP and HP heater bypasses; main feed
      pumps ×4; startup feed pumps ×2; feed control valves ×3 plus low-flow valves; crosstie
      XV-2A/2B; makeup
    Indications: Feed flow per SG and section; heater outlet temperatures; pump suction and
      discharge; deaerator level and pressure; hotwell levels; condensate conductivity and O₂; pump

* Electrical (EO)
    Controls: Switchyard breakers and isolators; generator, station and unit transformer breakers;
      11 kV incomers and bus sections; essential board incomers; EDG start, stop, sync, load;
      sequencer reset; UPS and charger lineups; trace heating feeders
    Indications: Voltage, current and frequency on every board; transformer temperatures and taps;

* Sodium services (NSO)
    Controls: Trace heating zones (≈ 180); cold traps; fill, drain and dump tanks; argon and

* Chemistry (CHO)

    Indications: Cover gas gamma spectrum; DND; sodium impurities and tritium; feedwater chemistry;

* Backup control room
    Controls: PSS and SSS trip; pony motors; DRACS dampers; SG relief valves; essential boards
    Indications: Trends for core outlet, pools, DRACS and flux

10.4 Crew sizes
---------------
* Minimum
    Positions: 3: SS, RO, field operator; everything else on auto
    What it can handle: Steady operation only; the first real fault will get away from them
* Standard
    Positions: 8: SS, RO, PO, SGO, TO-A, TO-B, EO, field
    What it can handle: Most scenarios
* Full
    Positions: 13: standard plus SSO, FWO, NSO, CHO, second field operator
    What it can handle: Everything; dispatcher can be a 14th player


10.5 Attention intervals on manual
----------------------------------
This is the check against AFK play. Each row is how long a variable stays in band with nobody
touching it.

* Main steam temperature
    Drifts out of band in about: 1–2 min
    Why: Frequency response, feed temperature noise, once-through sensitivity
* Header pressure and MWe
    Drifts out of band in about: Continuously while FCR-N is active
    Why: Grid frequency random walk
* P/Q
    Drifts out of band in about: Minutes, during any power change
    Why: Coupling
* Reheat balance
    Drifts out of band in about: 5 min after any split change
    Why: Coupling
* Cover gas pressure
    Drifts out of band in about: 5–10 min
    Why: Temperature and level changes
* Regulating rod band
    Drifts out of band in about: ≈ 2.7 h
    Why: Burnup drift at ×360: 0.75 pcm per real minute against a 250 pcm band
* Hydrogen background
    Drifts out of band in about: 30 min or more
    Why: Drift and meter noise
* Trace heating
    Drifts out of band in about: Random
    Why: Zone failures
* Deaerator, hotwell
    Drifts out of band in about: Stable
    Why: Buffered by design, as intended

Disturbance sources: feed temperature random walk ±0.5 K with 120 s time constant; CW intake drift
±1 K over grid-hours, plus summer upwelling steps of 5–10 K; grid frequency (§11.2); hydrogen meter
noise ±0.003 ppm; thermocouple noise ±1 K.


10.6 Hybrid control rooms
-------------------------
The 1988 analog panels stayed when the 2009 modernisation added screens, giving every room a hybrid
layout in the style of Temelín.

* Controls
    Analog, 1988: Rod, pump, breaker and valve switches; trip buttons; synchroscope
    Digital, 2009: None: screens never change plant state
* Indication
    Analog, 1988: Edgewise meters, chart recorders, mimic lines in system colours, lamp-box
      annunciators with first-out
    Digital, 2009: Plant computer: trends, core map, alarm list, sequence-of-events log
* Protection
    Analog, 1988: Trip status lamps
    Digital, 2009: Digital protection system displays and test panels
* Each room
    Analog, 1988: A large mimic board as the at-a-glance wall
    Digital, 2009: One or two operator screens per desk

Build rule: anything that changes plant state is a physical control on a panel. Screens show, sort
and trend.


10.7 Access, trust and griefing
-------------------------------
(Rule / Detail)
Access              : Any player can use any control, seated or standing, within reach of the panel
                      (≈ 10 studs) †. Proximity still matters, because the anti-solo design relies
                      on walking between rooms
Critical controls   : Manual reactor trip, SSS trip, turbine trips, opening 400 kV and 11 kV
                      breakers, loop drain, SG section dump and plant mode changes need a minimum
                      total playtime (suggested 3 h †)
Accountability      : Every control action is logged with player name and time in the
                      sequence-of-events log
Trips and vote-kick : After a player trips the plant, other players can vote-kick them, which bans
                      them from that server
Proposed safeguard  : The vote opens only if the server finds no trip condition, P1 alarm or
                      approaching setpoint at the moment of the trip, so a justified trip is never
                      punished. Majority of other players, minimum 3 voters †
Shifts              : None. Players stay on any desk for as long as they like. Where this spec says
                      shift, read session; the session report appears at the end of each grid day
                      and on request


10.8 Guides
-----------
Guide boards are dotted around the plant †.

* Desk card
    Where: At every desk
    Content: What the desk owns, its main controls, normal values, and which desks it has to talk to
* Alarm response card
    Where: Next to each annunciator panel
    Content: For each P1 and P2 alarm on that panel: what it means, first actions, who to call
* Field guide
    Where: At each local-only action point
    Content: What the action does, how long it takes, what to report back
* Room map
    Where: At each room entrance
    Content: Where the other rooms are and how long the walk is

Guides read their numbers from the same config module as the simulation, so they never go out of
date.
