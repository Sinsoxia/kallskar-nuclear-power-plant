7.3 Bypass
----------
Two-stage bypass on each turbine: HP bypass from main steam to cold reheat, and LP bypass from hot
reheat to the condenser, each sized for 60% of turbine flow, quick-opening in ≤ 2 s on a turbine
trip, with desuperheating. The condenser absorbs 60% bypass indefinitely at design cooling water;
beyond that, condenser pressure rises about 1 kPa per extra 10% †. Bypass buys time. Every kilogram
through it is output thrown away.

House-load operation: when the grid separates, one unit can run back to the plant's own house load
(≈ 65 MWe) with the rest of its steam on bypass, while the other unit trips and the reactor runs
back. Getting it right keeps the plant off its diesels (S-28).

7.6 Feedwater trains
--------------------
(Item / Value)
Train A and train B each           : 3 × 50% condensate extraction pumps; condensate polishing
                                     plant; 2 × 55% main feed pumps (12 MW motors on VFDs, 9.8 MW
                                     absorbed at 100%); 1 × 30% startup feed pump; HP heater bypass
Capacity                           : 2 main pumps 481 kg/s; with the startup pump 612 kg/s
Normal lineup                      : Train A feeds SG1 and half of SG2 (437.5 kg/s); train B feeds
                                     SG3 and the other half
Loop 2 crosstie                    : XV-2A and XV-2B let either train feed all of SG2
Train B lost                       : Train A must supply SG1 and SG2, 583 kg/s: both main pumps and
                                     the startup pump flat out (612 kg/s). Turbine A can only take
                                     437.5 kg/s, so the rest goes to bypass
Feed control                       : Per SG: main feed control valve plus low-flow valve, 10 s
                                     stroke; flow indicated per section
Chemistry (all-volatile treatment) : O₂ ≤ 5 ppb (alarm 10); pH 9.3–9.6; cation conductivity ≤ 0.2
                                     µS/cm (alarm 0.3); Na ≤ 3 ppb; Cl ≤ 2 ppb

8.4 Grid parameters: Nordic synchronous area
--------------------------------------------
(Parameter / Value)
Voltage                   : 400 kV ±5%; backup supply from the 130 kV regional network
Frequency                 : 50 Hz; normal band 49.9–50.1 Hz
Generator operating range : Continuous 49.0–51.0 Hz; time-limited down to 47.5 Hz and up to 51.5 Hz
Automatic load shedding   : From about 48.8 Hz
Scale of a K1 trip        : The grid is sized to ride through losing its largest unit, which is
                            bigger than K1. Losing both turbines at once is serious but survivable
Reactive capability       : +300 / −150 MVAr per unit at rated MW; less above 500 MWe in winter †

* FCR-N
    What it asks of the plant: Proportional response across 49.9–50.1 Hz; 63% within 60 s, 100%
      within 3 min
    K1 contract size †: ±10 MWe per unit
* FCR-D up
    What it asks of the plant: Adds power as frequency falls from 49.9 to 49.5 Hz; 50% within 5 s,
      100% within 30 s
    K1 contract size †: 25 MWe per unit, held as headroom below the unit's limit
* FCR-D down
    What it asks of the plant: Sheds power as frequency rises from 50.1 to 50.5 Hz; same speed
    K1 contract size †: 25 MWe per unit
* aFRR
    What it asks of the plant: Follows a setpoint signal from grid control
    K1 contract size †: ±20 MWe for the plant, full within 5 min
* mFRR
    What it asks of the plant: Manual instruction delivered within 15 min
    K1 contract size †: The dispatcher's normal MW instructions
